Main Settings

  • Traited Egg Rule: How this device grants a Traited Egg — a chance roll on Stage V and up, a win-ratio threshold, a timer since the last evolution, or outliving the pet's lifespan. #MODULETRAITEDEGGRULE
  • Power Bonus Rule: Where battle power above the species base comes from — full Strength Hearts paying a per-stage table, a flat amount per heart (to +16), the Digimon Mini's Strength bands (to +15), the level steps, the original Pendulum's effort stat, or Vital Bracelet stars. #MODULEPOWERBONUSRULE
  • Effort Power Tiers: For the Effort Tiers rule: the power a form's training adds, by stage and effort (one a training, reset on evolving). #MODULEEFFORTPOWERTIERS
  • Training Strength Gain: Strength given at each training outcome — Bad, Good, Great, Excellent. "full" means the meter is filled outright. #MODULETRAININGSTRENGTHGAIN
  • Training Weight Loss: Weight shed at each training outcome — Bad, Good, Great, Excellent. #MODULETRAININGWEIGHTLOSS
  • Training Effort Gain: Effort given at each training outcome. Most devices credit a failed training; the original Pendulum only credits a successful one. #MODULETRAININGEFFORTGAIN
  • Traited Egg Starting Level: Initial level for pets hatched from traited eggs. Higher levels provide stat bonuses including increased HP, power, and attack values. #MODULETRAITEDEGGLEVEL
  • Name Format: Template string for pet folder names. '$' is replaced with the pet's name. Example: "$_dmc" creates folders like "Agumon_dmc". Used for sprite loading paths. #MODULEFILEFORMAT
  • Visible Stats: Controls which statistics are displayed in the game UI. Allows customization of the information shown to players during gameplay. #MODULEVISIBLESTATS
  • Primary Sprite Format: The primary sprite format to load. Color uses 'monsters', Dot uses 'monsters_dot', HD uses 'monsters_hidef'. Falls back to secondary then the remaining format. #MODULEPRIMARYSPRITEFORMAT
  • Secondary Sprite Format: The secondary sprite format to try if primary is not found. Falls back to the remaining unselected format last. #MODULESECONDARYSPRITEFORMAT
  • Enable Special Attack Sprite: When enabled, critical attacks will use the 'Special' frame effect. #MODULEENABLESPECIALATTACKSPRITE
  • Count Evolution While Sleeping: When enabled, evolution counters will increase even while the pet is sleeping. When disabled, evolution progress only occurs during awake time. #MODULECOUNTEVOLUTIONWHILESLEEPING
  • Evolution Win Ratio Chance: The chance a route with a win-ratio criterion is taken, by the pet's win ratio: the row with the highest ratio at or below it applies. #MODULEEVOLUTIONWINRATIOCHANCE
  • Double Needs After Failed Evolution: A pet with a win-rate route whose evolution timer took no route never evolves on the timer again. With this on, it then drains hunger and strength and poops twice as fast, and so at Stage VI+ after 48 hours. #MODULEFAILEDEVOLUTIONDOUBLENEEDS
  • Reverse Attack Frames: Swaps attack animation frames (ATK1↔ATK2, TRAIN1↔TRAIN2) during sprite loading. Used for visual variety or correcting animation sequences in sprite sets. #MODULEREVERSEATKFRAMES

Training Settings

  • Battle Minigame: The battle minigame type used during battles. #MODULEBATTLEMINIGAME
  • Battle Protocol: The battle protocol is used to simulate a real device while connecting with non Omnipet devices. When set, Omnipet will emulate the selected device's communication protocol during battles. #MODULEBATTLEPROTOCOL

Battle Settings

  • Adventure Mode: Enables or disables adventure mode gameplay features. Required to add the module's Adventure Mode to the Battle Menu. #MODULEADVENTUREMODE
  • Adventure Style: The Adventure Style defines if the player can only move to the next battle or reset progress (like the DM20) or if the player can revisit any defeated area freely. #MODULEADVENTURESTYLE
  • Battle Cost Type: Which resource is required to battle. #MODULEBATTLECOSTTYPE
  • Battle Cost Amount: The amount of the battle resource will be consumed when battling. #MODULEBATTLECOSTAMOUNT
  • Battle Weight Loss: Weight in grams the pet loses after every battle, on top of the battle cost. 0 disables it. The original Digital Monster loses 4g per battle. #MODULEBATTLEWEIGHTLOSS
  • Training Costs As Battle: Every training costs the battle cost above (without the battle weight loss), and a pet that could not pay for a battle cannot train. The Digivice iC spends one hunger heart a battle or a training. #MODULETRAININGUSESBATTLECOST
  • Battle Item Use: When battle items (weapons, chips, disks) are used, from a list before the battle. Area: at the start of an adventure battle, lasting the item's boost time (the Digital Monster X's chips last the area). Every round: before each round, lasting that round (the Digivice iC's coliseum weapons). #MODULEBATTLEITEMUSE
  • Jogress Cost: What a Jogress costs the pets that take part. #MODULEJOGRESSCOST
  • Keep Battle Counts On Evolution: Keeps the battle record (battles, wins, Event Communications) across an evolution instead of clearing it. #MODULEBATTLEKEEPCOUNTS
  • Event Communication: The module has Event Communications (a connection that counts toward evolutions, as the Digimon Twin's). #MODULEEVENTCOMMUNICATION
  • Event Communication Outcomes: What an Event Communication can give, with the chance of each. Rows marked different versions need two pets of different versions. #MODULEEVENTOUTCOMES
  • Attribute Advantage As Power: The attribute advantage is added to Power before the hit rate is worked out, instead of to the hit rate (the Digital Monster X: +32 Power). #MODULEBATTLEADVANTAGEPOWER
  • Battle Panic Rate: Percent chance that taking a hit leaves the pet in Panic until the player mashes out of it (the Digimon Twin). 0 disables it. #MODULEBATTLEPANICRATE
  • Battle Panic Attribute Bonus: Percentage points a favourable attribute lowers the Panic rate by, an unfavourable one raises it. #MODULEBATTLEPANICBONUS
  • Base Sick Chance Win: Base percentage chance of getting sick after winning a battle. Modified by protein overdose and disturbance penalties. Minimum 5%, maximum 50%. #MODULEBATTLEBASESICKCHANCEWIN
  • Base Sick Chance Lose: Base percentage chance of getting sick after losing a battle. Modified by protein overdose (+10% per overdose) and disturbance penalties. Higher risk than winning. #MODULEBATTLEBASESICKCHANCELOSE
  • Injury Chance Step: Points a form's injury chance rises after each lost battle and falls after each won one (the Digivice iC: 1). #MODULEINJURYCHANCESTEP
  • Attribute Advantage: Percentage points added to the hit rate of the Digimon whose attribute has the advantage (Vaccine > Virus > Data > Vaccine) and taken from the other's, or added to Power where the module says so. #MODULEBATTLEATTRIBUTEADVANTAGE
  • Global Hit Points: When > 0, make the module ignore pets individual Health Points. It's also used to cap the possible damage taken. This attribute is mostly obsolete with the protocol battle system. #MODULEBATTLEGLOBALHITPOINTS
  • Sequential Rounds: Controls battle area progression mechanics. When enabled, battles progress through rounds sequentially rather than allowing random round selection. #MODULEBATTLESEQUENTIALROUNDS
  • Enable Feeding: This will allow the player to feed the pets after 3 battles (if area has exactly 5 rounds) or after 4 battles (if area has 6 or more rounds) following the DMX/PENZ pattern. Requires Battle Sequential Rounds to work. #MODULEBATTLEENABLEFEEDING

Care - Meat Settings

  • Weight Gain: Weight increase when feeding meat/hunger food. Applied only to pets above stage 1 and below 99 weight. #MODULECAREMEATWEIGHTGAIN
  • Hunger Gain: Amount of hunger restored per meat feeding. Pets can't eat when hunger equals stomach capacity or during overfeed timer. #MODULECAREMEATHUNGERGAIN
  • Care Mistake Time: Minutes of empty hunger before triggering a care mistake. Timer resets when pet is fed. #MODULECAREMEATCAREMISTAKETIME
  • Overfeed Timer: Minutes pet refuses food after trying to overfeed. Pet will display 'nope' animation during this time. #MODULECAREOVERFEEDTIMER
  • Condition Heart: When enabled, uses condition hearts system instead of mistake counter. Each care mistake reduces condition hearts. #MODULECARECONDITIONHEART
  • Can Eat Sleeping: Allows pets to eat food while in nap state. When disabled, feeding attempts during sleep will fail. #MODULECARECANEATSLEEPING
  • Back to Sleep Time: Minutes before pet automatically returns to sleep after being woken up during sleep hours. #MODULECAREBACKTOSLEEPTIME
  • Enable Shaken Egg: Allows eggs to be "shaken" (shake_counter >= 99) for evolution bonuses. Can provide a power bonus. #MODULECARESHAKENEGG
  • Flush Disturbance Sleep: When enabled, using flush while pet is sleeping counts as a sleep disturbance. #MODULECAREFLUSHDETURBANCESLEEP
  • Fixed 4 Hearts: When enabled, hunger and vitamin meters always show 4 hearts regardless of pet's stomach attribute. #MODULECAREFIXED4HEARTS
  • Poop Chance: Probability distribution for poop types (Single, Double, Triple, Giga). Percentages must sum to 100%. #MODULECAREPOPCHANCE
  • Poop Alarm: When enabled, pet will call for attention when it needs to poop. Helps prevent care mistakes. #MODULECAREPOOPALARM
  • 99g Effect: Defines what happens when a pet reaches 99g of weight. #MODULECARE99GEFFECT
  • Poop Sickness Count: How many poops on screen needed for the pet to get sick. #MODULECAREPOOPSICKNESSCOUNT
  • Poop Sickness Effect: Defines the visual effect when the pet gets sick from poop. #MODULECAREPOOPSICKNESSEFFECT
  • Block Actions When Sleeping: When the pet is sleepy or asleep, it won't be possible to battle, feed or train. #MODULECAREBLOCKACTIONSSLEEPING
  • Can Battle While Sick: When enabled, the pet can battle even while sick. When disabled, battling is blocked until the pet is healed. #MODULECARECANBATTLEWHILESICK
  • Can Battle While Injured: When enabled, the pet can battle even while injured (Skull). When disabled, battling is blocked until the injury is healed. #MODULECARECANBATTLEWHILEINJURED
  • Allow Below Min Weight: When enabled, weight losses can take a pet below its species' Min Weight, down to 1. When disabled, Min Weight is the floor. #MODULECAREALLOWBELOWMINWEIGHT
  • Needs Drain While Sleeping: Hunger and strength keep draining while the pet sleeps. #MODULECARENEEDSDECAYSLEEPING
  • Can Train While Sick: A sick pet may still train (battling while sick is its own setting). #MODULECARECANTRAINSICK
  • Disturbance Sickness Count: Every this many sleep disturbances the pet falls sick. 0 disables it. #MODULECAREDISTURBANCESICKNESSCOUNT
  • Disturbance Sickness Effect: The sickness those disturbances bring: Dots, Skull or Nothing. #MODULECAREDISTURBANCESICKNESSEFFECT
  • Poop Mistake Time: Minutes a poop may stay uncleaned before it costs a care mistake and a point of virus. 0: never. #MODULECAREPOOPMISTAKETIME

Care - Protein Settings

  • Weight Gain: Weight increase when feeding protein/strength food. Applied only to pets above stage 1 and below 99 weight. #MODULECAREPROTEINWEIGHTGAIN
  • Strength Gain: Amount of strength restored per protein feeding. Strength depletes over time and empty strength triggers care mistakes. #MODULECAREPROTEINSTRENGTHGAIN
  • DP Gain: DP (stamina) gained every 4th protein feeding if current DP is below max energy. Consumed during battles. #MODULECAREPROTEINDPGAIN
  • Care Mistake Time: Minutes of empty strength before triggering a care mistake. Timer resets when pet receives protein. #MODULECAREPROTEINCAREMISTAKETIME
  • Overdose Max: Maximum protein overdose counter that affects battle sick chance. Each protein feeding increases overdose. #MODULECAREPROTEINOVERDOSEMAX
  • Strength Max: The most Strength a pet holds, whatever its stomach. Protein past it is still eaten and still adds weight. 0: the stomach is the cap. #MODULECARESTRENGTHMAX
  • Protein Penalty: Determines the amount of each penalty for Protein Overdose in percent. Affects battle sick chance. #MODULECAREPROTEINPENALTY
  • Disturbance Penalty Max: Maximum penalty from sleep disturbances that affects battle sick chance. Each disturbance adds 2 to penalty. #MODULECARESDISTURBANCEPENALTY
  • Sleep Care Mistake Timer: Minutes before triggering a care mistake for sleep neglect. Timer resets when pet gets proper sleep. #MODULECARESLEEPCAREMISTAKE

Death Settings

  • Max Injuries: Injuries (Skull) in one form that kill: battles and the Skull causes. #MODULEDEATHMAXINJURIES
  • Max Sicknesses: Sicknesses (Dots) in one form that kill, counted apart from injuries. #MODULEDEATHMAXSICKNESSES
  • Care Mistake: Total care mistakes before death. Includes hunger, strength, sleep, and sickness mistakes. #MODULEDEATHCAREMISTAKE
  • Sick Timer: Minutes a sickness (Dots) may go untreated, awake, before the pet dies. #MODULEDEATHSICKTIMER
  • Injury Timer: Minutes an injury (Skull) may go untreated, awake, before the pet dies. #MODULEDEATHINJURYTIMER
  • Hunger Timer: Minutes of empty hunger before death. Death occurs after continuous hunger neglect exceeding this threshold. #MODULEDEATHHUNGERMISTAKE
  • Starvation Count: Number of hunger depletion cycles before death. Each cycle occurs when hunger reaches 0 and isn't refilled. #MODULEDEATHSTARVATIONCOUNT
  • Debilitation Count: Strength depletion cycles with the meter empty before death; filling the Strength meter completely resets it (the Xros Wars Color: 18). 0 disables it. #MODULEDEATHDEBILITATIONCOUNT
  • Strength Timer: Minutes of empty strength before death. Represents weakness from lack of protein/training. #MODULEDEATHSTRENGTHTIMER
  • Stage 4-5 Mistake: Mistakes threshold for stage 4-5 pets after evolution time expires. Represents adult-stage care requirements. #MODULEDEATHSTAGE45MISTAKE
  • Stage 6-7 Mistake: Mistakes threshold for stage 6+ pets after 48 hours of age. Expert care required for long-term survival. #MODULEDEATHSTAGE67MISTAKE
  • Death Save By: How a dying pet can be saved: B presses, shakes, or Nothing (it dies). #MODULEDEATHSAVEBY
  • Death Save Amount: Presses or shakes the save needs. #MODULEDEATHSAVEAMOUNT
  • Death Save Time: Seconds to make them in; out of time, the pet dies. #MODULEDEATHSAVESECONDS
  • Death Save Chance: Chance (%) the pet lives once they are made. #MODULEDEATHSAVECHANCE
  • Lifespan Rule: How a pet's life ends: None (it lives on), Activity Time (a form that cannot evolve 2 hours after its evolution time goes near-death), Old Age (it dies Death by Old Age days into its life) or Age 5 (from 5 days old Hunger and poop come twice as fast, and 16 care mistakes in one form kill). #MODULELIFESPANRULE
  • Death by Old Age: The Old Age rule's days: the pet dies this many days into its life, counted asleep and awake. #MODULEDEATHOLDAGE

Vital Values & Item Boosts

  • Vital Value Base: Minimum Vital Values gained per hour if well fed and not sick. Represents the natural recovery rate in good health. #MODULEVITALVALUEBASE
  • Vital Value Loss: Vital Values lost per minute if hungry or sick. Higher values make pets deteriorate faster when neglected. #MODULEVITALVALUELOSS
  • HP Max Item Boost: Maximum HP boost from items. 0 means only temporary boost items will work. #MODULEHPMAXITEMBOOST
  • Attack Max Item Boost: Maximum attack boost from items. 0 means only temporary boost items will work. #MODULEATKMAXITEMBOOST
  • Power Max Item Boost: Maximum power boost from items. 0 means only temporary boost items will work. #MODULEPOWERMAXITEMBOOST

Reliability Settings

  • Reliability Base: Starting Reliability for a new first-generation pet. 0 disables the stat. #MODULERELIABILITYBASE
  • Becoming Full: Reliability change when feeding makes the pet reach maximum Hunger. #MODULERELIABILITYBECOMINGFULL
  • Answering Call: Reliability change when the player answers a pet call. #MODULERELIABILITYANSWERINGCALL
  • Ignoring Call: Reliability change when a pet call expires unanswered. #MODULERELIABILITYIGNORINGCALL
  • Minigame Success: Reliability change when a minigame reports a successful result. #MODULERELIABILITYMINIGAMESUCCESS
  • Two Poops: Reliability change for every two accumulated, uncleaned poops. #MODULERELIABILITYTWOPOOPS
  • Sickness or Injury: Reliability change whenever sickness or injury begins. #MODULERELIABILITYSICKNESSORINJURY
  • Battle Win: Reliability change after each battle victory. #MODULERELIABILITYBATTLEWIN
  • Hourly: Reliability change every 60 elapsed minutes. #MODULERELIABILITYHOURLY

G-Cell Settings

  • G-Cell Style: The G-Cell evolution system: Disabled; DMGZ, one 0-472 point meter paid by flat values; or PENGZ, 14 cells sized by stage (Red from 8) paid by stage. #MODULEUSEGCELLS
  • Points Per Cell (PENGZ): Points each of the 14 cells holds, by stage (Stage 0 to VI+). #MODULEGCELLPOINTSPERCELL
  • Care Mistake (PENGZ): Points a care mistake or the empty-strength call costs, by stage. #MODULEGCELLCAREMISTAKEBYSTAGE
  • Battle Win / Loss (PENGZ): Points a won / lost battle pays, by stage. #MODULEGCELLBATTLEBYSTAGE
  • Random Encounter Win / Loss (PENGZ): Points a won / lost random encounter pays, by stage. #MODULEGCELLENCOUNTERBYSTAGE
  • Training Gain (PENGZ): Points a training pays by its grade (Bad, Good, Great, Excellent); with Anguirus on the second line. #MODULEGCELLTRAININGGAIN
  • Other Device Win / Loss (PENGZ): Points a versus against another module's pet pays. #MODULEGCELLOTHERDEVICE
  • Random Encounter Win: G-Cell points gained when winning random encounters. Default: +12. #MODULEGCELLRANDOMENCOUNTERWIN
  • Random Encounter Lose: G-Cell points lost when losing random encounters. Default: -6. #MODULEGCELLRANDOMENCOUNTERLOOSE
  • Battle Win: G-Cell points gained when winning regular battles. Default: +6. #MODULEGCELLBATTLEWIN
  • Battle Lose: G-Cell points lost when losing regular battles. Default: -4. #MODULEGCELLBATTLELOOSE
  • Training Success: G-Cell points gained from successful training. Rewards training skill development. #MODULEGCELLTRAININGSUCCESS
  • Training Phase2 Failure: G-Cell points gained from advanced training failure. Default: +2. #MODULEGCELLTRAININGPHASE2FAILURE
  • Training Phase1 Failure: G-Cell points lost from basic training failure. Default: -2. #MODULEGCELLTRAININGPHASE1FAILURE
  • Protein: G-Cell points lost when feeding protein. Default: -4. #MODULEGCELLPROTEIN
  • Care Mistake: G-Cell points lost per care mistake. Default: -8. Largest G-Cell penalty. #MODULEGCELLCAREMISTAKE
